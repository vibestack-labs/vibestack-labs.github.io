Privacy Policy — Vibestack Labs

Developer: Vibestack Labs  |  Effective Date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games and applications developed and published by Vibestack Labs (including Trapshift and other casual mobile titles). We respect your privacy and design our games to be safe and transparent.

1. Information We Collect

Vibestack Labs does not require user accounts and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location) to our own servers.

  • Local Device Storage: Game progress, high scores, unlocked cosmetics, and settings are stored strictly locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our free-to-play games use Google AdMob to display optional, voluntary rewarded video advertisements or standard ad formats. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers and usage metrics (such as the Google Advertising ID, IP address, device model, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

Learn more about how Google handles data in partner apps here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information through third-party ad networks, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

4. Data Security & Deletion

Because all gameplay save data stays locally on your device, you can delete your data anytime by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the game.
